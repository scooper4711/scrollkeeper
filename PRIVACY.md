# Scrollkeeper Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. This policy covers Scrollkeeper for Mac and iPad.

**Scrollkeeper collects no data.** It has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
third-party code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

## Your Paizo account

Scrollkeeper signs in to paizo.com with the email address and password you give it, to list the digital titles you
own and download them. Your email address and password are kept in the Keychain on your device and go only to
Paizo. The sign-in session is kept in memory and ends when the app closes. Paizo learns what any sign-in and
download tells it, under Paizo's own privacy policy; the developer sees none of it.

Remove Account, in Settings, removes your email address and password from the Keychain. The demo library needs no
account and contacts no one.

## What stays on your device

- **Your library list, cover pictures, descriptions and tags** are saved on your device.
- **The files you download** are saved on your device, in the folder you choose. They are never uploaded.
- **Your settings**, such as the view you last used, are saved on your device.

Use Remove Account before you delete the app: deleting it can leave your email address and password in the
Keychain. Deleting the app from an iPad deletes the rest. On a Mac, macOS keeps the app's saved data in the
Containers folder of your Library folder until you delete the folder named Scrollkeeper there, and your downloads
stay in the folder they were saved to.

## Network

Scrollkeeper contacts Paizo's servers (and the servers Paizo's store sends it to for files and cover pictures) to
sign in, read your library and download titles. It contacts no one else. Links you click, such as Paizo's Community
Use Policy, open in your browser, under the browser's and that website's own privacy policies.

## Other apps on your Mac

On the Mac, the downloads and the list of your titles are kept in a folder Scrollkeeper shares with the developer's
other apps (Pluck, Mapsmith, Pawn Shop, Field Guide and Turnkeeper), so they can open the titles you own. Those apps
can also ask Scrollkeeper to download a title. All of this stays on your Mac; each app's own policy covers what it
does.

## The disk image version

The version downloaded from GitHub can check GitHub for a newer release when it opens, if you leave that setting
on. The check sends GitHub the same request any web browser would; the developer sees nothing of it.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send crash reports for
Scrollkeeper to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the
app.

## Children

Scrollkeeper collects no data from anyone, including children.
